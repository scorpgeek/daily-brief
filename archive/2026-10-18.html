Global Trade

Trump waives the 100% Russia-oil tariff on India

Executive order late Friday invokes §115(a) of the Graham Act to exempt India and China from duties on being among the top five buyers of Russian oil and gas. The threat had been scheduled to bite on Oct 18 — today.

Why it matters Clears the pre-Christmas shipping window. Russian crude was 44% of India's August imports. Rubio visits Mumbai and Jamnagar next; the waiver lands six weeks before the US midterms.
Breaking

17 killed in stampede at a Kolkata Durga Puja pandal

A sudden shower Saturday evening sent people rushing for shelter inside the pandal. The crowd surge was uncontrolled. More than 25 injured and taken to hospital; CM Suvendu Adhikari confirmed the toll, which is feared to rise.

Why it matters Modi announced ex-gratia compensation for the families. Details on the pandal structure and crowd management are still to come.
Technology

AI deepfakes are swamping the protest coverage

Sexually suggestive AI clips, protest footage cut into sexualised montages, forged documents attributed to protest leaders, and old footage relabelled as live — all viral across Instagram, Facebook and X amid the Delhi-NCR and Mumbai agitation against the SIR.

Why it matters Detection is losing. Specialised tools caught ~75% of AI images in early 2023, only ~40% by late 2025. Demand for verification at pi-labs is up over 100% quarter on quarter.
Markets

Q2 banking splits: ICICI +20%, HDFC barely grows

HDFC Bank's net profit rose just 4.7% to ₹19,510 cr as NIM slipped to 3.16% and bond yields hardened — a market cap that has fallen 27% in a year. ICICI Bank grew profit 20% to ₹14,843 cr. YES Bank's headline +65% becomes a ₹5,217 cr pro-forma loss once the AT1 ruling lands.

Why it matters Anup Bagchi takes over HDFC as MD & CEO this month, ending the succession standoff. Deposits are being priced with 6.5% FCNR(B) money and it is compressing everyone's margins.
Healthcare

30% margin cap on 137 cancer drugs, effective Nov 9

NPPA notified the cap on trade margins Saturday, covering Atezolizumab, Bevacizumab, Daratumumab and others across all strengths and formulations. Government claims prices fall up to 70%, saving patients ₹2,500 cr a year.

Why it matters Keytruda, the immunotherapy drug, is not on the list. The Supreme Court had called the existing markups "broad daylight dacoity" — drugs bought at ₹2,700 sold at ₹27,000.

02Trade & Energy

●●●Trade

The waiver buys time, not diversification

Exporters are relieved ahead of Christmas but the strategic shift is already locked in. Royal Classic Mills cut US exposure from 85% to 40% in fifteen months — the US is still its largest market, then Australia, Canada, the EU and the UK. "Don't put all our eggs in one basket."

Gopalakrishnan, R Gopalakrishnan / Royal Classic Mills
●●FTA

India's first tariff cuts under the New Zealand FTA

Effective Oct 20. Zero BCD on specified sheep meat, fish, wine and furniture; manuka honey at 51%, kiwi at 15%. TRQs set for honey (40 tonnes) and albumins (200 tonnes). Dairy and specified agricultural goods are excluded. Bilateral goods trade was ~$1.13 bn in 2025-26.

Department of Revenue notification
●●Energy

Long-term LNG contracts are being planned. India became the first official partner country for Russia Energy Week in Moscow, with eight oil companies on the India pavilion. Discussions covered oil and gas, mining, the steel value chain and Far East coking coal.

03Policy & Regulation

●●●Drugs

What the cancer drug cap actually says

30% of MRP excluding GST — a 42.86% mark-up on the stockist price — applied across all strengths, dosage forms and combinations. Revised MRP is computed off March 2026 stockist prices; government sales excluded. Six months' notice to withdraw. Keytruda is excluded.

Notified by NPPA, Saturday
●●Courts

Reservation expansion runs into the Supreme Court

SC upheld the Madras HC ruling that converting from Hinduism does not entitle anyone to SC or OBC quota — killing Tamil Nadu's 3.5% Muslim OBC quota. Separately the Karnataka HC struck down a 2022 BJP-era SC reservation law, and Madhya Pradesh moved the SC against a HC ruling that capped its OBC quota at 27%.

Three states, three separate legal tracks

04Healthcare

●●●Deep Dive

The markup chain nobody can see

Petitioner's data: doxorubicin 50mg is ₹250 at Jan Aushadhi against an average of ₹1,230 at top retail brands. Gemcitabine 1,000mg: ₹631 versus ₹6,412. Bortezomib: ₹3,188 versus ₹11,411. Average markup 170%, highest 700%. Scheduled drugs are only 17% of a ₹2.59 lakh crore market.

Kishan Chand Jain's court filings
●●●Deep Dive

Why the cap probably won't reach most patients

Patented drugs cost ₹1.5–2 lakh a vial and their price is not driven by markups at all. Margin caps should be calculated on ex-factory price, not printed MRP. 1.5 million new cancer cases a year; ₹7.5 lakh average treatment cost. Tata Memorial and the National Cancer Grid's pooled procurement show the alternative — savings of 23% to 99%.

Max Healthcare, HCG, Jan Swasthya Abhiyan respond

Mayank Bathwal, Aditya Birla Health Insurance
