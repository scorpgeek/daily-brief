Archive past editions

Dated editions were piling up loose in the project root, and there was no
way to find last Tuesday without opening the folder and guessing.

- build_brief.py writes each edition to archive/ instead of the root, so
  the root only ever holds the launcher, the index page, and the two
  published aliases
- every build updates archive/manifest.json with one line per edition:
  date, paper, pages, mode, lead story. A browser cannot enumerate a
  directory, so the list has to live in something it can fetch
- ARCHIVE.html lists them by month, filterable by date, paper or headline,
  with phone and laptop links per day
- linked from the launcher, next to the Today button

Two honest degradations rather than fabricated data:

- an edition with no manifest record shows a dash instead of a guessed
  newspaper and page count. Sunday's edition predates the manifest, and
  parsing those figures back out of generated HTML would mean putting
  numbers in the index that I cannot verify
- archive/ is gitignored, so on the published site the fetch 404s and the
  page says so and links to today. An archive listing editions it cannot
  open is worse than one admitting it has none

Archived editions keep whatever design they were built with; Sunday's
predates the band layout and still renders in the older card format.

The tests no longer hardcode a date — they follow the newest archived
edition plus the aliases, so a template change that breaks an older file
is caught too.
