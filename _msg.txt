Let search results dismiss themselves

Results survived until you deleted the letters from the box, so a stale
panel sat over the document after you had already navigated away. Only
clicking a result closed it.

- Escape closes results
- a click anywhere outside the search box and the results closes them,
  which also covers rail index links and glossary terms without either
  needing a handler of its own
- clicking a result clears the box as well as the panel, rather than
  leaving the term sitting there with no results under it
- Enter opens the first result; typing a term and pressing Enter was a
  reflex that did nothing

Escape now peels one layer at a time: results first, glossary drawer
second, so a search open on top of the drawer does not close both at
once.
