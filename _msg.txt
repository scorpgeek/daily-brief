Publish the archive

archive/ was gitignored on the reasoning that those files reproduce
article text. The user wants the history public, so it is now tracked and
served.

- dropped the archive/ ignore and anchored the root-only dated-file rules
  with a leading slash, which was silently swallowing archive/ too
- committed the two past editions and archive/manifest.json
- the launcher note and the archive empty state no longer claim the
  editions are unpublished, and the launcher note stays hidden until a
  probe actually fails

The two date rules were a live hazard: gitignore patterns without a slash
match at any depth, so "20??-??-??.html" was already covering
archive/2026-10-19.html. Untracking the folder without anchoring them would
have looked like it worked while committing nothing.

archive/ grows by roughly 140 KB a day. If that matters, prune old editions
rather than untracking the folder -- an index listing days whose files are
missing is worse than a short archive.
