Add a back-to-top control to both editions

Seventeen folds stack a long way down, the reading column runs 74
characters wide, and neither device has a useful Home key. There was no
shortcut from the bottom of the page to the top.

- a button appears once you are past the first screen, fades over the
  same 120ms the disclosure chevron uses, and scrolls to the top
- hidden at the top and inert, so it never covers the masthead
- on the laptop it steps aside when the glossary drawer is open rather
  than landing underneath it
- the desk had no smooth scrolling at all; it does now, and the
  reduced-motion override is fixed in both templates

Two bugs came out of this. The reduced-motion rule in the desk template
had one brace too few, so the entire back-to-top block ended up nested
inside that media query and applied only when the reader had reduced
motion switched on -- zero rules applied for everyone else. The phone
had the same rule written as a selector nested inside a declaration
list, which is invalid CSS and was dropped silently. node --check only
parses JS, so neither was caught by the build.

test_classes.py now walks the stylesheet for unbalanced braces and for
selectors nested inside declaration lists, and test_css_parser.py proves
that check fires on both of those exact shapes. A lint that never fails
reads as coverage and is not.
