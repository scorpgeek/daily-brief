Fix search, which had been dead since the templates were cut

doSearch() threw on res.innerHTML and #res did not exist. When the
templates were reduced to marker files the main content block was
replaced wholesale, and <div class="res" id="res"> went with it. The
search JS and the complete .res CSS both survived, so the failure looked
like a styling problem rather than a missing node, and every existing
check stayed green.

- restored the results container at the top of the reading column
- results now quote the enclosing block, not the matching text node; a
  glossary term is its own node, so searching GCC showed a bare "GCC"
  with no sentence around it
- one row per block rather than one per occurrence
- section labels read "01  Top 5" instead of "01Top 5WATCH 4 ·"
- snippets are escaped, so article text containing & or < cannot break
  the markup
- the results panel excludes itself from the search, or a second search
  matched the first search's output

test_classes.py now checks that every getElementById and querySelector
target in the inline JS resolves to a real element. That is the check
that would have caught this.
