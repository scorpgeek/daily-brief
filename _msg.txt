Stop printing the date twice

The rail carried "Mon 19 Oct 2026 / ET Delhi" and the sticky bar carried
"Monday, 19 October 2026 / ECONOMIC TIMES - DELHI" right beside it. Both
surfaces are sticky, so the duplication was permanent rather than
something you saw only at the top, and the two formats made it look
like a rendering fault rather than a decision.

- the rail keeps the document identity: paper, date, pages, mode
- the bar now shows position instead: current section, its number, how
  far through the document you are, and a progress hairline
- that is what a sticky bar is for, and it removes the duplication
  rather than restyling it
- the rail header is now sticky within the rail, since the bar no longer
  carries the date and the rail scrolls on a short viewport
- the last section could never become current because there is not enough
  page left to scroll it past the marker, so the bar read 20/21 at the
  very bottom; it now pins to the last section once the bottom is reached
